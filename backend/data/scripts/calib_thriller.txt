BEAT 1: A locked server room. A countdown already running.
RHEA: Ninety seconds before the door seals. Talk fast.
NOOR: The threat isn't outside. It's on the badge you're holding.

BEAT 2: The setup tightens. A gun on the table nobody reaches for yet.
RHEA: You planted the danger and then warned me. Why?
NOOR: Because I need you alive to open what I can't.

BEAT 3: A reversal, earned and sharp.
RHEA: The blood on the floor is yours. You're the one who's trapped.
NOOR: Then we're trapped together. Fifty seconds.

BEAT 4: The ticking clock, footsteps in the shadow, a scream cut short.
RHEA: Someone's in the vault with us.
A knife scrapes the dark. Silence. A single heartbeat, too loud.

BEAT 5: The reveal lands because it was planted in beat one.
NOOR: The badge. It was always the badge. You've been the key the whole time.
RHEA: Then the door was never the trap. I was.

BEAT 6: Cliffhanger on the hook. The seal hisses shut.
RHEA: Ten seconds. Tell me what opens it.
NOOR: You already know. You've known since the rooftop.
The lights die. A phone rings in the dark. Don't hang up.
