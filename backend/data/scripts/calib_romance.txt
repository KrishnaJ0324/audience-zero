BEAT 1: A rooftop at dusk. Two people who shouldn't want each other.
AISHA: I keep finding reasons to walk past your window.
KABIR: I keep leaving the light on so you will.

BEAT 2: The slow burn. Nothing happens and everything does.
AISHA: Tell me one true thing.
KABIR: When you laugh I forget the argument I was rehearsing.

BEAT 3: A tender misstep. The longing tightens.
AISHA: If I stay tonight I won't be able to pretend tomorrow.
KABIR: Then don't pretend. Stay.
Their hands find each other in the dark.

BEAT 4: The payoff. The confession years in the making.
KABIR: I loved you before I had the word for it.
AISHA: I know. I was waiting for you to catch up to me.

BEAT 5: Quiet aftermath, hearts open, promise made.
AISHA: Whatever comes, we go to it together.
KABIR: Together. I promise. I finally get to promise.

BEAT 6: Morning. A knock. The world returns but the warmth holds.
AISHA: Let it wait. Ten more minutes of just us.
KABIR: Ten more minutes. Then the whole rest of our lives.
